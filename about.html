About RePurpose!

We have all heard the common sustainability practices like "turn off the lights when you leave a room" and "say no to plastic bags", but what about the single use items we have piled at home? People often throw away useful household items because they don't realize they can be repurposed. This leads to heavy accumulation in landfills and leaks chemicals and waste into the environment, deteriorating the ecosystem's stability. By recycling and repurposing the items we would have thrown away, we can create an enormous difference.

This is why I created this website. By allowing people to have countless personalized craft ideas at their fingertips, we can further implement SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production practices by encouraging people to extend the life of everyday items and develop more sustainable habits.

So what are you waiting for? Start recycling! One craft at a time
